FICTIONAL EXERCISE / NOT A REAL EVENT

One rainfall figure, two different baselines

A district average and a monthly average can tell different stories.

Analysis draftResearch: 3 November 2026Political alignment: unassessed

01 / WHAT IS ESTABLISHED

Begin with the evidence.

A fictional district records 120 mm of rain. A 100 mm monthly baseline makes that 20% above average; a 150 mm seasonal comparison makes it 20% below. These comparisons answer different questions. Neither establishes a trend.

Fictional premise

120 mm is 20% above a 100 mm baseline.

No supporting real-world evidence is supplied.
Not established

This one observation proves a long-term rainfall trend.

03 / WHO GETS TO SPEAK

Selection shapes the story.

Look for patients, residents and domain experts alongside officials. This exercise supplies no real interview record; the presence or absence of voices is a question for further reporting.

04 / CONTEXT & TIMELINE

Keep the comparison in bounds.

Check that time period, geography and denominator match before comparing headlines.

Sampling and translation notes

Three deliberately selected documents illustrate differences in editorial purpose. This is not a representative sample or a political coverage distribution. Translations retain the original headline and require separate Hindi review.
