News & research / Ganga cleaning

Haridwar’s Ganga clean-up: clearer water, then fresh litter reports

Compare the canal clean-up, reports of returning waste and what the visuals cannot establish.

Research draft · English and Hindi editorial review pending · Updated 18 November 2026. Source reports are dated 11–16 November.

What the reporting establishes

Reporting describes waste removal around Haridwar during the Upper Ganga Canal’s annual maintenance closure. The Indian Express dates the closure from October 20–21 until water returned on November 7. This was a specific canal-fed stretch, not a clean-up of the entire Ganga. Source

Later reports describe litter returning and officials proposing further measures. The reports appeared on different days. A clearer-looking stretch and subsequent litter are not, by themselves, contradictory accounts of the same moment. Source

What still needs evidence

  • What do dated water-quality measurements at the same locations show before and after the closure?
  • Which waste-removal and year-round maintenance figures have supporting records?
  • How much waste is prevented from entering the water, rather than removed after it arrives?

We have not independently measured water quality, audited the waste tonnage, verified the viral footage or established that any new penalty was introduced. Planned interventions and completed outcomes must be kept separate.
