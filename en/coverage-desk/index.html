What happens before publication

  1. A publisher date and original URL are saved separately from collection time, with a hash of the observed headline.
  2. Same-language titles within two days are compared conservatively. Generic wording and incompatible numbers do not establish a match. Cross-language matches need editorial work.
  3. An editor must confirm the event match and examine full-text evidence. Repeated releases from one publisher still count as one publisher.
  4. Opening an article in Qwen starts the existing private review flow only when you submit it. It does not approve the group or publish the model’s response. The shared free-plan allowance still applies.

The index retains up to 2,000 records from 30 days. Matching examines at most the latest 250 records from seven days. Public Git history keeps prior snapshots. Article bodies are not stored in this index.
