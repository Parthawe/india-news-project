Published selection and review rules

The pilot requires at least 3 eligible sources in each Left and Right cohort, fresh and healthy feeds, continuous collection from the event’s start, approved same-event matching and completed wire-origin review. A signal requires at least 3 covering groups, a cohort coverage rate no higher than 20%, the opposite rate at least 60%, and a gap of at least 40 percentage points. These are pilot screening rules, not proof of motive or a scientific definition of bias.
