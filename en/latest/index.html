The publishing pipeline

New links arrive automatically. Assessments need review.

  1. Approved feeds supply unchanged headlines, source links, authors and publication dates. Each source has a recorded reuse policy.
  2. Canonical links and hashes remove exact duplicates. Missing dates, future dates, failed retrievals and out-of-scope hosts are excluded.
  3. Thirty days of metadata remain in a bounded public archive. Similar headlines form provisional groups; they do not establish the same event or political blindspots.
  4. AI can prepare evidence-linked drafts within the existing model budget. This collector makes no model calls. Editors must approve full comparisons and translations before editorial publication.
  5. Feed health, source timestamps and unchanged-language headlines remain visible. The five-item official daily brief is a separate selection.
