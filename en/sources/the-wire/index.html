Political leaning

Provisional Left / centre-left

The sampled authored arguments oppose Hindu majoritarian historical narratives and centralisation, while the reporting repeatedly foregrounds civil liberties, minority and Indigenous rights, and constraints on executive or corporate power. This supports a provisional Left/centre-left tendency in this Indian sample. Ordinary scrutiny of government is not, on its own, evidence of Left alignment. Economics and national strategic autonomy do not fit a uniform Left/Right template.

Sample size and eligibility rules

Qualifying annotated sample: 12 articles, 6 topics, 9 bylines.

Our pilot floor is 12 distinct articles across 3 topics and 2 bylines within 180 days, with news and opinion identified. Meeting the floor does not assign a rating: India and edition scope, an evidence-backed rationale and an editorial approval record are also required.

The assessment and its limits

English web articles, 12 purposively selected pieces from July–November 2026, spanning current reporting and authored analysis. No automatic transfer to Hindi, Urdu or other editions. Left here includes centre-left constitutional and social politics.
