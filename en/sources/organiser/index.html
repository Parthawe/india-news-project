Political leaning

Provisional Right

The sampled constitutional commentary defends the RSS against Congress and the Left; cultural essays root public values in Hindu civilisational traditions; national-security and economic reporting use a national-strength frame. Attributed RSS quotations alone are not treated as endorsement. The judgment rests on the combination of authorial constitutional/cultural argument and repeated selection/framing, not ownership or a factuality score.

Sample size and eligibility rules

Qualifying annotated sample: 12 articles, 6 topics, 9 bylines.

Our pilot floor is 12 distinct articles across 3 topics and 2 bylines within 180 days, with news and opinion identified. Meeting the floor does not assign a rating: India and edition scope, an evidence-backed rationale and an editorial approval record are also required.

The assessment and its limits

English web edition; purposive 12-article baseline from 26 November 2026, not a probability sample or a full-history rating.

Provisional AI editorial assessment, not human-reviewed. A purposive baseline cannot estimate every article or contributor. News, opinion and attributed claims remain separate. This is not a factuality score.

An expired or changed assessment is excluded from automated blindspot counts. The sample is a baseline for further review, not a permanent newsroom identity.
