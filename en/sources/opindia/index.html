Political leaning

Provisional Right

The authorial language repeatedly defends Hindu political/religious interests, endorses Modi-government narratives and describes secular, left-wing or opposition actors with hostile ideological labels. The judgment concerns the sampled English feed. It does not independently verify the articles’ allegations, funding connections or legal interpretations, and it does not imply that government support and Right are the same dimension.

Sample size and eligibility rules

Qualifying annotated sample: 12 articles, 9 topics, 7 bylines.

Our pilot floor is 12 distinct articles across 3 topics and 2 bylines within 180 days, with news and opinion identified. Meeting the floor does not assign a rating: India and edition scope, an evidence-backed rationale and an editorial approval record are also required.
