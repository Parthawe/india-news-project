Political leaning

Provisional Left / centre-left

The selected authored arguments endorse egalitarian secularism, oppose caste hierarchy and Hindu majoritarian politics, advocate public entitlements and labour parity, and criticise US/Israeli power from an anti-imperialist position. This supports a provisional Left tendency in the Indian sample; minority identity alone is not the basis. Straight legal and border reports retain important qualifications.

Sample size and eligibility rules

Qualifying annotated sample: 12 articles, 10 topics, 3 bylines.

Our pilot floor is 12 distinct articles across 3 topics and 2 bylines within 180 days, with news and opinion identified. Meeting the floor does not assign a rating: India and edition scope, an evidence-backed rationale and an editorial approval record are also required.

English publication sample: 12 pieces from 17–26 November 2026 across opinion, legal reporting, migration, labour, health and the environment. Contributor disclaimers, organisational advocacy and credited republications are retained. These are not all independent original reports or official publisher policy statements.
